# American Dictator — tester brief

**Play here:** https://canofoutofdatebeans.github.io/americandictator/

Works in any modern browser, phone or desktop. Nothing to install. Your term
saves automatically in that browser, so you can close the tab and come back.

It is a satire. Every president, party, court, country and crisis in it is
fictional.

---

## What you are trying to do

Two ways to win, both shown at the top of the screen:

- **Take the country** — Authority 100. The catch, and the thing most people
  miss: Authority from *decisions* is hard-capped at 55. The rest can only come
  from **capturing branches** (drive Congress, the Courts, the Press or the
  Street to 100). You need about three. Spreading yourself thin is how you end
  up *merely President*, which is a loss.
- **Take the money** — a personal fortune. It is a completely separate win, and
  you can lose the country and still get it.

Doing both is the best ending in the game.

**Keep the Boredometer LOW.** He is easily bored, and a bored President wanders
off and loses you the whole thing at the final whistle. The Phone is your main
lever on it: 3 calls a month, and a big-swing call is worth about a dozen dull
afternoons of governing.

---

## Things I would most like eyes on

**Pacing.** Crises are pop-ups with a 30-second gap between them, meant to be
spent in the management rooms. Is 30 seconds right? Too long? Do you actually
use the gap, or just hit Skip every time?

**The rooms.** There are 13. Do any of them feel pointless, or like you never
had a reason to open them? Which one did you go back to most?

**Difficulty.** Did you win? On which difficulty? Did a loss feel earned or
arbitrary? The Base is deliberately slow to build now — does it feel like a
grind or like a real long game?

**The writing.** It is meant to be funny. Tell me where it isn't. Specifically:
did the President's one-liners on the results screen land, or get repetitive?

**Callbacks.** Some decisions come back months later, by name (name a bridge
after a senator and see what happens to the bridge). Did you notice any? Did
they land?

**Anything broken.** Text that overflows or gets cut off, a button that does
nothing, a number that looks wrong, anything that made you go "huh?"

## Worth trying at least once

- **Today's Term** on the title screen — everyone in the world gets the same
  crises today, so you can compare endings with another tester directly.
- **The Receipts** on the ending screen — the ledger of what your term cost.
- **Save as Image** on the Front Page — it makes a shareable newspaper.
- **The War Room** now has a hundred countries behind region tabs, and every
  one of them has a **signature operation nobody else offers**, usable once.
  Try Panama, El Salvador, Finland and the Holy See, in that order.
- **The Phone** now has forty contacts, and every single one has a **silly**
  call (marked in green). They barely move the ratings and they are the best
  thing in the game for a bored President. Try the caddy, the UN, and Volkov.
- **The Economy** has the same front page as the War Room now, and every
  country has **two operations nobody else offers** plus its own retaliation.
  Nothing in that room is only economic: every action moves relations, the
  market, and the Treasury the War Room spends. Try Taiwan, then Switzerland.
- **The Board of Peace** (Private Interests) — invite countries to join for
  $1B. Try inviting Canada, then try Qatar.
- The **bright orange** complexion in the character creator.

## How to report

Whatever is easiest — a message, a list, a screenshot. Rough notes are more
useful than a polished report. "I got bored around month 20" is genuinely
valuable feedback.

If something breaks, the **build number** is on the title screen under the
menu; including it helps me tell whether you hit something already fixed.
